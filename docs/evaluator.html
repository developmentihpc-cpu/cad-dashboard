Step 4

Weighting & evaluation

Weights: 100%
Scoring uses AI — Claude reads the full proposal and scores each criterion with reasoning & cited sources. If the scoring server is offline (dot turns red), a provisional rule-based draft (keyword & data rules) is used automatically instead.
Adjust any weight before scoring — subtotals and the composite update live. Criteria tagged Knockout fail the project (standard review) if scored below their minimum.
Scoring guide — what the numbers mean
85–100Strong — fully addressed, robust evidence (risk: negligible)
65–84Good — well addressed, minor gaps (risk: low)
45–64Moderate — partially addressed, notable gaps (risk: moderate)
25–44Weak — barely addressed, significant gaps (risk: high)
0–24Poor — not addressed / strong negative evidence (risk: severe)
Risk criteria are inverted: a higher score means lower risk.
